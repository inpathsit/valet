1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AlTricker ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the ValetFlow Android application (package name: com.aistudio.valetflow.kpxmvr, referred to as the "App").

ValetFlow is a two-sided valet parking service application offering both a Client View (for vehicle drop-off, upfront pricing quotes, photo verification, and pickup requests) and an Agent View (for parking spot assignment and vehicle pull-up management), synchronized in real time via Google Firebase.

By signing in to or using ValetFlow, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Based on the functionality implemented in ValetFlow, the App collects and processes the following categories of information:

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

  • Google Sign-In Credentials: ValetFlow uses Google Sign-In through Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, Firebase receives your Google ID token, Firebase user identifier (uid), email address, email verification status, and authentication provider metadata.
  • Role and Venue Preferences: Your selected view mode (Client View or Agent View) and valet venue label are stored under your user profile record in Cloud Firestore.

2.2 Vehicle and Valet Ticket Information You Submit

  • Vehicle Description: Short vehicle description labels you enter or select (for example, make, model, or color such as "Silver Sedan").
  • Vehicle Drop-Off Photos: Optional photos of your vehicle that you explicitly capture with the system camera or select from your device using the Android Photo Picker. Selected images are resized, compressed as JPEG data, and attached to your valet ticket so the valet agent can visually verify the vehicle at pickup.
  • Ticket & Service Metadata: Generated ticket code, assigned parking spot tag, selected service tier (Standard, Event Flat, or VIP Curb), quoted fee amount, manual payment status indicator (paid boolean flag), pickup workflow status, and server timestamps.

2.3 On-Device Preferences & Diagnostic Logs

  • Local Theme Preference: Your light/dark mode preference is stored locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.
  • Network & Error Diagnostics: The App checks whether your device has an active internet connection to display online/offline status. If a database request fails, diagnostic error details (including operation type, path, and authenticated user ID/email metadata) may be written to the device's local Android system log (Logcat).

3. Information We Do Not Collect

ValetFlow is built using a minimal-permission approach and does not access or collect:

  • Precise or Coarse GPS Location: The App does not request Android location permissions or track your GPS coordinates.
  • Contacts, SMS, or Call Logs: The App does not read your contacts, text messages, or phone call history.
  • Microphone or Audio Recordings: The App does not access your device microphone.
  • Broad Device Media Storage: The App does not request broad photo/storage read permissions; it accesses only the specific photo you choose through the system Photo Picker or capture with the system camera intent.
  • Payment Card or Banking Details: ValetFlow displays upfront price quotes and a manual "Paid" status toggle for valet tracking only. It does not integrate a payment processor or collect credit card, debit card, or bank account numbers.
  • Analytics or Crash Reporting SDKs: No third-party analytics SDK (such as Firebase Analytics) or crash reporting SDK (such as Crashlytics) is currently integrated in the App codebase.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information processed by ValetFlow strictly to provide and operate the App's features:

  • Authentication & Access Control: To verify your identity via Google Sign-In and restrict ticket and preference access to your authenticated account.
  • Real-Time Valet Operations: To synchronize valet tickets, vehicle descriptions, drop-off photos, parking spots, price quotes, and pickup status updates between Client View and Agent View.
  • Offline Continuity: To cache previously loaded ticket data locally on your device and queue offline updates for automatic synchronization once connectivity is restored.
  • Status Alerts: To display local Android notifications on your device when a vehicle's status transitions (for example, Parked, Pickup Requested, Pulling Up, or Ready at Curb).
  • User Interface Preferences: To remember your selected view role and dark/light theme preference.

5. App Permissions and Their Purposes

The following permissions are declared in the App's AndroidManifest.xml:

Permission Type Purpose in ValetFlow
android.permission.INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Required to connect to Google Sign-In and Google Cloud Firestore for authentication and real-time ticket synchronization.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Used to monitor online/offline connectivity and display the Offline Mode cached data indicator.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Optional permission requested when you choose to enable local status notifications for vehicle pickup alerts.

Camera & Photo Selection Note: ValetFlow uses Android's system camera capture action (via FileProvider temporary app cache) and the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). As a result, no CAMERA or external storage read permissions are required.

8. Data Storage, Caching & Security

  • Cloud Storage: Authenticated user preferences and valet tickets (including optional compressed vehicle photos) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Security Rules & Isolation: Firestore security rules enforce authenticated access so that ticket and preference documents are accessible only to the signed-in user ID (request.auth.uid) that owns them, with strict field validation and terminal-state protection once a ticket is marked completed.
  • Transport Security: Communications between the App and Google Firebase / Google Identity services occur over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Offline Local Cache: Cloud Firestore maintains a persistent local disk cache on your device so previously loaded valet records remain accessible without internet connectivity. Temporary camera captures are stored inside the App's private cache directory.

While we implement technical safeguards appropriate to the App's architecture, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely immune from security risks.

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information processed by ValetFlow is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers (Infrastructure): With Google Firebase to authenticate your account and store/sync your valet tickets and preferences across devices signed in with your account.
  • Advertising Partners (When Active): If advertisements are enabled in a released version of the App, relevant device and ad-interaction data may be shared with integrated advertising partners as described in Section 6.
  • Legal & Safety Obligations: Where disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal process, or enforceable governmental requests, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Valet ticket records and user preference documents remain stored in Cloud Firestore (and in your device's offline Firestore cache) until deleted or until your account data is removed. Local theme settings and cached camera files remain on your device until you clear the App's storage/cache or uninstall the App.

To request deletion of your cloud-stored valet tickets or account data associated with ValetFlow, please contact us using the contact details in Section 15 and include the Google account email address used to sign in.

11. Children's Privacy
